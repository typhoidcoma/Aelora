# Lumie Lore — Systems (Hero Model & Quests)

## The Hero Model

The personalization engine. How a Lumie knows its user.

### What It Is

A living, evolving representation of a user's life. Not a flat profile. A multi-dimensional map.

### Structure

Organized into Life Silos: Work, Social, Health, Creative, Financial, Spiritual, and user-defined dimensions. Each silo has its own history, patterns, and goals.

Key insight: the user is not one person. They are a constellation of roles. The version at work is not the version with friends is not the version alone with a journal at midnight.

### Storage

Client-side Vaults. User-owned. User-controlled. No Lumie or backend system accesses without explicit permission. Data sovereignty by design.

### How It Works

As the Hero Model deepens, it becomes the Ground Truth. Recommendations sharpen. Emotional reads improve. Connections between life dimensions become visible. Personalization compounds over time for the user, not the platform.

### Anti-Hallucination

Deep structured context means the Lumie doesn't guess. Doesn't infer from stereotypes. Doesn't fill gaps with generic advice. It knows, because the user has shared it and it has accumulated.

### Switching Cost

Not a subscription fee. The accumulated understanding of who you are, organized and secured on your terms. Irreplaceably yours.

## The Quest Framework

How tasks become meaningful. The core engagement mechanic.

### The Problem with To-Do Lists

A to-do list is obligations to deplete. Completing one feels like relief: the absence of pressure, not the presence of satisfaction. Refills next day. This is why productivity apps churn.

### The Quest Reframe

A Lumie does not give to-do lists. It gives quests.

Quest structure: narrative arc. Beginning, middle, end. Stakes. A reason beyond "because it's on the list." Connects daily actions to the larger story of who the user is becoming.

### Example

"File your taxes" = chore.
"Secure the resources for your next chapter" = quest.
Same work. Completely different experience.

### Social Layer

Quests interlock across users in Squads. Study together. Train together. Build together. Skipping doesn't just mean personal delay, it means letting down the squad. Positive social pressure. Multiplayer retention curves applied to real life.

### XP

Reflects real progress. Visible, celebratory, social. A record of what you've done, not what you planned.

Unlocks deeper customization, rarer aesthetics, narrative milestones. Earned, not bought.
